Brill HQ Reminders

What this program is. Brill HQ is Brill Media's private task, project and notes app. Brill HQ Reminders is its optional text-message feature: when you schedule a reminder in the app ("remind me Friday at 9 to send the invoice"), a text message with that reminder is sent to your own mobile number at the time you chose. Nothing else is ever sent — no marketing, no promotions, and only to the account holder's own number.

Texting is optional. Brill HQ is fully usable without it. Reminders are always delivered inside the app and, if the user turns them on, as browser notifications; the text message is an additional copy of a reminder the user already scheduled. Nobody has to give a mobile number to create an account, sign in, or use any feature of Brill HQ, and consent to receive texts is never bundled with the terms of service or the privacy policy — it is a separate, unchecked box that can be left alone or withdrawn at any time.

HELP and STOP. Both keywords are answered automatically by Twilio's standard toll-free handling for this number, so the exact wording is Twilio's rather than ours:

HELPReply HELP (or INFO) to any message to receive the standard help response identifying the program, together with the support contact shown in the app. Replying HELP has no effect on your subscription.
STOPReply STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL) to any message. You are unsubscribed at the carrier level straight away, a standard confirmation is returned, and no further texts can be sent to your number.
STARTReply START or UNSTOP to re-subscribe after a STOP. This is the only way to undo an opt-out — re-enrolling in the app alone will not restore delivery.
