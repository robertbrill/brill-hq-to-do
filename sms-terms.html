Brill HQ Reminders — SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. Program description

Brill HQ Reminders is an optional text-message feature of Brill HQ, Brill Media's private task, project and notes application. When an account holder schedules a reminder in the app, a text message containing that reminder is sent to the mobile number they enrolled, at the time they chose. The program sends only the reminders you create. It never sends marketing or promotional content.

2. How you enroll

You can enroll (a) in the app under Reminders → Get reminders by text by entering your mobile number and ticking the consent box, or (b) by texting the keyword REMIND to the Brill HQ Reminders number from the phone you added in the app. In both cases we send a confirmation text and you must reply YES before any reminders are sent. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing anything.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends entirely on how many reminders you schedule: one text per reminder, and one per occurrence for a repeating reminder.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Brill Media does not charge for this program.

5. Cancelling (opt out)

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one final text confirming that you have been unsubscribed, after which no further texts are sent. You can also turn texts off at any time in the app under Reminders. To re-enroll, reply START or enroll again in the app.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message for program information, or contact the account owner through the Brill HQ app.

7. Supported carriers & liability

The program is available on major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your network operator.

8. Privacy

Your mobile number and consent records are used only to operate this program. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy for details.

We may update these terms from time to time; the current version is always available at this page. Continued participation after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
